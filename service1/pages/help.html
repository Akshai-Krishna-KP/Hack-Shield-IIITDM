Frequently asked questions

I can't sign in to my account +

Check that your username is correct and that Caps Lock is off. If you still cannot sign in, contact your IT administrator.

How do I keep my account secure? +

Use a unique password, never share your sign-in details, and report unexpected account activity promptly.

How can I find a colleague? +

Open the employee directory from your workspace to browse colleagues and departments.

Where can I find company information? +

Visit the company information page for an overview of Northstar and its workplace resources.
