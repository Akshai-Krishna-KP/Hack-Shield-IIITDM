Incident response, made simple

A calm, clear process helps us resolve issues quickly and keep the right people informed.

Northstar IT Operations · Updated October 26, 2026

1. Identify and report

Record what happened, when it started, and which systems or teams appear affected. Share observable facts and avoid speculation.

2. Notify the right team

Contact the on-call owner and use the established incident channel. For suspected security issues, notify the Security team promptly.

3. Keep a clear timeline

Capture key actions, decisions, and timestamps. A concise timeline helps responders coordinate and makes follow-up easier.

Remember

Prioritize user safety and service recovery. Do not share credentials, secrets, or sensitive customer information in public channels.

4. Review and learn

After recovery, document the root cause, contributing factors, and practical actions that reduce the chance of recurrence.
